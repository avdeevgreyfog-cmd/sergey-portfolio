# Portfolio / orbital direction

Personal developer portfolio for Sergey Avdeev. Audience: prospective clients who need to understand the offer, inspect existing work, and discuss a project.

## Design contract
- Thesis: the relationship between design and implementation, expressed by two overlapping orbital bodies, with a clear, readable presentation beside them.
- Signature: a single commissioned/generated obsidian orbital sculpture matching the supplied dark blue references. Decorative only; never product evidence. Reused as a quiet crop in the contact panel.
- Palette: deep ink #050a10, navy surface #0b1420, porcelain text #f3f6fc, slate text #acb8ca, line #263344, ice accent #b6c7ff.
- Typography: self-hosted Manrope variable, Cyrillic and Latin subsets, SIL Open Font License alongside font files. Compact 600 display, 400 body, 500 interface labels. No serif substitutions.
- Grid: maximum 1200px, 48px desktop margins; hero text and image have independent columns. Mobile: text, actions, sculpture, compact capability rail. Projects live exclusively under Works.
- Surfaces: 7–9px radius on content panels, thin blue-grey borders; pills only navigation/filter controls. No repeated floating shadows.
- Imagery: supplied project capture and clearly labelled demonstration data for Operations OS; no invented project brands, achievements or endorsements.
- Motion: short hover and navigation transitions; content visible without JavaScript; reduced-motion supported.

## Reference review
- User's five attached dark orbital layouts: composition, material, low-noise surfaces; fictional projects and biographical claims excluded.
- https://www.exoape.com/ : restrained navigation, large confident imagery, clear text hierarchy (visually inspected).
- https://activetheory.net/ : inspected, interactive experience reports unsupported browser. No claims about full experience.
